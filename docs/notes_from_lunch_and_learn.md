## Session: Navigating Professional Interviews

Speaker: Renuka Deshmukh - Software Data Engineer at 
Date: May 8th, 2026

### Key Takeaways

- Maintain eye contact during interviews to build rapport and show confidence.
- Prepare for behavioral questions by reflecting on past experiences and using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure responses.
- Research the company and role thoroughly to tailor your answers and demonstrate genuine interest.
- Practice coding problems regularly to improve problem-solving skills and speed.
- Be honest about your skills and experiences; it's better to admit when you don't know something than to try to bluff your way through it.
- Follow up with a thank-you email after the interview to express gratitude and reiterate your interest in the position.
